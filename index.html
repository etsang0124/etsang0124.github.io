Algorithmic Disintermediation

How Software Replaces the Broker: The Recommendation Engine

Human real estate brokers charge 15% of annual rent to manually filter inventory. SkyLoft replaces this manual friction with a mathematical scoring engine designed for urban transit networks.

1. Multi-Objective Commute Decay

Rather than relying on linear geographic distance, the engine computes door-to-door travel duration using NYC MTA transit graphs. Commutes under 25 minutes receive maximum fit weight, with an exponential decay curve applied past 35 minutes.

Score = w_commute · e^(-λ · max(0, t - 25)) + w_budget · (1 - |Rent - Target| / Target)

2. Concession Amortization Math

NYC landlords frequently offer marketing concessions (e.g., "1.5 months free on a 14-month lease"). The engine programmatically calculates the Net Effective Rent to allow true apples-to-apples price comparison while strictly preserving legal gross lease terms.

Net_Effective = Gross_Rent · (Lease_Months - Free_Months) / Lease_Months

3. Controlled Constraint Relaxation

When high-demand filters produce zero exact matches, the system dynamically relaxes constraints in tiered order (move-in date window first, followed by a 10% budget expansion) to present nearby high-fit alternatives without abandoning the user session.

Tier 1: Date ±14 days → Tier 2: Budget +10% → Tier 3: Adjacent Subway Line
